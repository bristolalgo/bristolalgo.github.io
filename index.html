About the group

We are the Algorithms and Complexity research group in the School of Computer Science at the University of Bristol . Our research covers algorithms, computational complexity and related areas of theoretical computer science.

The group works on theoretical questions relating to the design, analysis and limitations of algorithms, including problems involving large data sets, graphs, strings and computational complexity.

Research interests

  • 02 Massive-data algorithms, streaming, communication complexity and distributed computing — Christian Konrad
  • 03 Counting and parameterised complexity, randomised algorithms, random processes on networks and graph theory — John Lapinskas
  • 04 Computational complexity — Shuo Pang

Undergraduate and MEng projects

We offer BSc/MEng projects for Bristol students, including algorithm implementation, engineering and theory. Students interested in algorithmic theory and with a strong mathematical foundation are encouraged to get in touch.
